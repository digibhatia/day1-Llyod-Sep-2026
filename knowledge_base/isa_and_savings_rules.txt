LLOYDS FINANCIAL (TRAINING SCENARIO) — ISA AND SAVINGS ACCOUNT RULES
Document ID: POL-SAV-009 | Last reviewed: this is synthetic training content, not a real policy

1. Individual Savings Account (ISA) allowance
   - The total ISA subscription limit follows the current UK tax-year
     allowance, split across cash ISA, stocks and shares ISA, and
     innovative finance ISA as the customer chooses.
   - Transfers from a previous tax year's ISA do not count against the
     current year's allowance.

2. Cash ISA
   - Variable and fixed-rate cash ISA products are available.
   - Fixed-rate cash ISAs require funds to be locked in for the stated term
     (1, 2, or 3 years); early access incurs an interest penalty equivalent
     to 90 to 180 days of interest depending on the term.

3. Easy access savings
   - No withdrawal limits or notice periods.
   - Interest is calculated daily and paid monthly or annually, as selected
     by the customer at account opening.

4. Regular saver account
   - Monthly deposits between £25 and £250.
   - A preferential interest rate applies only while the linked current
     account remains open and in good standing.
