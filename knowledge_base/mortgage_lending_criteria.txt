LLOYDS FINANCIAL (TRAINING SCENARIO) — RESIDENTIAL MORTGAGE LENDING CRITERIA
Document ID: POL-MTG-014 | Last reviewed: this is synthetic training content, not a real policy

1. Eligibility
   - Applicants must be a UK resident aged 18 or over.
   - Minimum deposit: 10% of property value for standard residential mortgages.
   - Maximum loan-to-value (LTV): 90% for standard applicants; 95% under the
     assisted first-time-buyer scheme.

2. Affordability
   - Maximum loan amount is capped at 4.5x sole or joint annual gross income.
   - A stress-tested affordability check is applied at the Bank of England base
     rate plus 3 percentage points.
   - Existing unsecured debt repayments are deducted from usable income before
     the multiple is applied.

3. Term and rate
   - Standard mortgage terms range from 5 to 35 years.
   - Fixed-rate products are available for 2, 3, 5, or 10-year initial periods.
   - Early repayment charges apply during the fixed-rate period, typically
     1% to 5% of the outstanding balance depending on the product.

4. Documentation required
   - Three months of payslips or two years of accounts for self-employed
     applicants.
   - Three months of bank statements.
   - Proof of deposit source (savings, gift letter, or property sale).
