LLOYDS FINANCIAL (TRAINING SCENARIO) — CURRENT ACCOUNT OVERDRAFT AND FEES
Document ID: POL-ACC-022 | Last reviewed: this is synthetic training content, not a real policy

1. Arranged overdraft
   - Interest rate: 39.9% EAR (variable) on arranged overdraft balances.
   - Customers can apply for an arranged overdraft limit between £100 and
     £5,000 through online banking, subject to an affordability check.
   - No daily or monthly usage fee is charged for arranged overdrafts.

2. Unarranged overdraft
   - If a payment takes the account beyond an arranged limit (or into
     negative balance with no arranged limit), it is treated as unarranged.
   - No unarranged overdraft fee is charged, but the same 39.9% EAR interest
     rate applies to the unarranged portion of the balance.
   - Customers are notified by app or SMS alert before an unarranged
     overdraft interest charge is applied where possible.

3. Grace period
   - A 30-day grace period applies to newly opened student and graduate
     accounts, during which overdraft interest is charged at 0%.

4. Account fees
   - Standard current accounts: no monthly fee.
   - Premium packaged account: £17/month, including travel and phone
     insurance and a preferential overdraft rate of 19.9% EAR.
