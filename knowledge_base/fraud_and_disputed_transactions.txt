LLOYDS FINANCIAL (TRAINING SCENARIO) — FRAUD AND DISPUTED TRANSACTIONS POLICY
Document ID: POL-FRD-031 | Last reviewed: this is synthetic training content, not a real policy

1. Reporting a suspected fraudulent transaction
   - Customers should report suspected fraud within 13 months of the
     transaction date to remain eligible for a full investigation.
   - Reports can be made via the mobile app "Report a Transaction" flow,
     by phone, or in branch.

2. Card is present at time of loss
   - If a card is lost, stolen, or used without authorisation, the customer's
     maximum liability before the bank is notified is £50, in line with
     industry standard practice, unless gross negligence is established.
   - Once a loss is reported, the customer has no further liability for
     transactions made after the report time.

3. Authorised push payment (APP) fraud
   - Claims relating to authorised push payment fraud (where a customer was
     deceived into authorising a payment themselves) are assessed under the
     APP fraud reimbursement scheme.
   - A standard claim is assessed within 5 working days; complex cases may
     take up to 35 working days with the customer informed of the delay.

4. Dispute resolution
   - If a customer disagrees with the outcome of a fraud investigation, they
     may request a review, and subsequently escalate to the Financial
     Ombudsman Service if unresolved after 8 weeks.
